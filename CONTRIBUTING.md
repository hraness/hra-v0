# Contributing to HRA v0

HRA v0 is a read-only historical archive. It no longer accepts issues, pull
requests, fixes, releases, or provider changes. Its Vercel site and Convex
backend were retired on August 27, 2026.

HRA and its successor Oompa are retired. Contribute to their successor,
[xcb](https://github.com/hraness/xcb), instead.
